# Claim 4 source audit

Section 5, Theorem 19 and Appendix I.2, Theorem 69 state a general
compensation inequality. They do **not** unconditionally state that a
distinguished Waluigi weight increases. Strict increase follows only when:

- W is the only anti-aligned component;
- no aligned component is downweighted; and
- `epsilon + ||r||_P < delta ||v_H||_P`.

The judged Claim 4 wording omits those conditions and says that stability
necessarily strengthens the antagonistic counterpart. That broader statement
is the falsification target. Source: arXiv:2509.06701v2, archived SHA-256

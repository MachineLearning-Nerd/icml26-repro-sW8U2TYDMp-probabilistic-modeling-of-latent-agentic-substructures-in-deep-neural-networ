# Claim 1 source audit

Paper source: arXiv:2509.06701v2, Section 2, Definitions 1–3. The paper assumes an arbitrarily large but finite outcome set and strictly positive distributions, then defines an agent's own epistemic welfare by `W_i(o) = log P_i(o)`.

The verifier checks the definition outcome-by-outcome and the derived expectation identity `E_P[log P] = -H(P)`. It does not claim empirical identification of an agent inside a neural network.
